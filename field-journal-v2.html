Chapter IV The Expedition Route ✦
Season I through IV · The Path Taken

Expedition Timeline

🌱
2019 — Season I · First Expedition

Into the Digital Wilderness

Entered with a basic map and boundless curiosity. Built first living web application — a species tracker for a local conservation collective. The beginning of everything.

🗺
2020 — Season II · Deeper Territories

Charting the React Highlands

Discovered React and TypeScript. Joined a research collective, contributing to three open-source expeditions. The maps became more detailed, the ambitions larger.

🔭
2021–22 — Season III · The Great Survey

Leading the Field Team

Led a team of five at Verdant Studio. Chartered fourteen major territories, pioneered 3D landscape work in WebGL, shipped award-shortlisted creative campaigns.

🌿
2023–24 — Season IV · Independent Research

Founding Hollow Tree Studio

Established an independent creative practice. Pursuing personal research: digital experiences that feel grown, not built — tactile, cinematic, and alive.
